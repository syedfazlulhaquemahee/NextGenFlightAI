Popular help articles

Quick answers to the questions travelers ask most.

Why did the price change after I searched?+

Travel inventory changes quickly. A fare is confirmed only after the provider accepts the reservation at checkout.

How do cancellations and refunds work?+

Changes and refunds depend on the airline or provider’s fare rules. Start in Manage booking to see the options available for your itinerary.

Where can I find my booking confirmation?+

Use your booking reference in Manage booking. Your confirmation is also sent to the email address used at checkout.

I need help signing in to my account.+

Use the password reset option on the sign-in screen. For account access issues, contact support from the email tied to your account.
